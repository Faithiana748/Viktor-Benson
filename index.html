About Me

I'm Viktor Benson — a content creator, entrepreneur, digital marketer, and Chemical Engineer. I am building businesses around one simple belief:

Audience + Value = Money

Over the years, I've built audiences, created content, launched digital businesses, experimented with different online business models, and learned from both success and failure.

Today, I use everything I've learned to build a personal brand and companies that help people learn, create, connect, and build.

Through my content, I share practical lessons on content creation, business, entrepreneurship, digital marketing, personal growth, and my journey of building in public.

Through Coursearly, I'm building infrastructure that helps creators and experts turn their knowledge into courses and digital products. Through MyBlud, I'm building tools and experiences around communities, helping people create paid communities where audiences can connect, learn, and grow together.

And through this website and my newsletter, I bring everything together — my ideas, experiences, businesses, resources, and lessons from the journey.

My goal isn't simply to tell people what to do. It is to build useful things, share what I learn, and help more people turn their knowledge, skills, and experiences into something valuable.
